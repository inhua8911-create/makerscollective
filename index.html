About us

The Makers Collective began with a simple idea: talented makers should spend their time making. We take care of photography, listing, marketing and sales. Creators receive an agreed amount for each product and can earn extra incentives based on terms we agree together.

Every piece you see here is handmade in small batches by a real person.
